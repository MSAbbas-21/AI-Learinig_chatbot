💡 Why does this matter in AI?

When you ask an AI model "How many 'r's are in strawberry?", it doesn't see s - t - r - a - w - b - e - r - r - y. It sees token IDs like [straw, berry]. Because the characters are compressed inside the token, the AI has to guess or perform multi-step reasoning to count internal letters!

📖 The Complete Guide: How AI Actually Works

A crystal-clear, intuitive roadmap to the mathematics and computer science behind modern AI.

01

The Core Concept: Next-Token Prediction

At their core, Large Language Models (LLMs) are not conscious thinking entities. They are probabilistic sequence completion engines.

Given a sequence of words $w_1, w_2, \dots, w_t$, the model calculates a probability distribution over its entire vocabulary to predict what token $w_{t+1}$ should come next.

P(next_word | previous_words)
02

From Text to Numbers: Tokenization

Computers cannot multiply letters. Text must be converted to numbers:

  • Vocabulary: A master dictionary of 32,000 to 256,000 subwords.
  • Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE): Breaks rare or long words into subwords.
  • Token IDs: Each subword receives a permanent integer ID.
03

Vectors & Embeddings: Geometry of Meaning

A word ID is just an arbitrary index. To give it meaning, the model maps each ID to an Embedding Vector—a list of hundreds or thousands of floating point numbers.

In this high-dimensional geometry, similar concepts cluster together, and relationships like King - Man + Woman = Queen emerge organically.

04

The Transformer Engine: Self-Attention

How does a word know its context? In "The bank of the river", how does "bank" know it's not a financial bank?

Self-Attention solves this by computing three vectors for every token:

  • Query ($Q$): What am I looking for?
  • Key ($K$): What do I offer?
  • Value ($V$): What information do I hold?
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(Q·Kᵀ / √d) · V
05

How Neural Networks Learn: Loss & Backprop

Training happens in iterative loops across billions of words:

  1. Forward Pass: The model guesses the next word.
  2. Loss Function: Cross-Entropy loss calculates how wrong the guess was.
  3. Backpropagation: The chain rule of calculus calculates gradients for every weight.
  4. Optimizer (AdamW): Weights are nudged in the direction that lowers the loss.
06

The 3 Stages of Modern AI Creation

1. Pre-training: Reading trillions of internet tokens. Teaches language, logic, and common sense. Produces a Base Model.
2. Instruction Tuning (SFT): Training on high-quality Q&A pairs to transform the raw text predictor into an assistant.
3. RLHF / Alignment: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback. Teaches the bot to be helpful, honest, and harmless.
